Instant Large File View (Read-Only Preview)

✨ "Oh, I can open a 2.5 GB CSV in 200 milliseconds without freezing my laptop just to inspect the schema?!"
The Pain Point

You download a 2.5 GB database dump or server log. Opening it in Excel freezes your entire system or crashes with an "Out of memory" error.

Writing a Python script or booting a notebook with Pandas just to check the column names and inspect a few sample rows takes minutes to parse and consumes gigabytes of RAM.

The NanoCell Solution

NanoCell opens multi-gigabyte files instantly in $O(1)$ time. A background Web Worker samples the header row, regular intervals across the file, and the footer without parsing the full file.

It automatically engages View-Only Mode (locking edits) so you can safely verify schemas, column counts, and sample records with zero risk of file corruption and zero RAM strain.

🛡️ Strict Text Fidelity

Zero Data Corruption: Verbatim Text Accuracy

✨ "Oh, my zip codes won't lose leading zeros, phone numbers keep their '+', and SKU numbers don't turn into scientific floats?!"
The Pain Point

You open a customer list, order table, or inventory export in a standard spreadsheet. The application tries to be "smart" by guessing data types.

Zip codes like "02138" lose their leading zero and become 2138. Phone numbers like "+44 7911..." lose their +. Product codes like "84E02" become 8.40E+03. When you save, your original database identifiers are permanently destroyed.

The NanoCell Solution

CSV is text, and NanoCell handles every single cell as pure verbatim text. Zero unwanted type casting, zero loss of leading zeros or signs.

Pasting raw delimited text fills the grid predictably without triggering broken delimiter split dialogs or auto-formatting accidents. What you see is exactly what gets written back to disk.

International Phone "+44 7911 123456" 447911123456 (+ sign lost!) +44 7911 123456 (Exact string)
Product Part SKU "9876E5" 9.88E+08 (Corrupted to float!) 9876E5 (Untouched identifier)
ISO Timestamp String "2026-05-12" 12/05/2026 (Forced localized rewrite) 2026-05-12 (Clean ISO preserved)
⚙️
In Settings (Ctrl+G): You can set your preferred delimiter (comma, semicolon, tab, or pipe), choose text encodings like UTF-8, enforce strict quotation rules, and automatically remove empty rows and columns when opening files.
🧹 1-Click Database Prep

Data Validation: Clean Messy CSVs For SQL Import

✨ "Oh, I can auto-clean messy headers, invalid commas, and mixed international dates for database import in 10 seconds?!"
The Pain Point

You need to bulk import a CSV into PostgreSQL (\COPY), Snowflake, or MySQL (LOAD DATA). The pipeline crashes halfway through.

The culprit: column headers contain spaces or illegal characters (e.g. "Order Total ($)"), two columns share the same name, cells have invisible trailing spaces, European decimal numbers use commas (19,95), or mixed international dates swap months and days.

The NanoCell Solution

Run Validate Headers or Validate Data. NanoCell scans the entire dataset and displays proposed fixes in the virtualized Validation Pane.

Review proposed changes side-by-side and click Accept All to execute every fix in a single atomic transaction that can be undone in one Ctrl+Z!

Visual Example: Validation Rules & Proposed Cell Transformations Validation Engine
Issue Category Original Value (Dirty) Sanitized Value (Proposed Fix)
Header Syntax "Customer First-Name" customer_first_name (Converted to clean SQL identifier)
Duplicate Header "total" (Second occurrence) total_c4 (Auto-disambiguated with column index)
Whitespace Trimming "  Paris, France  " "Paris, France" (Padded whitespace stripped)
Decimal Comma Coercion "149,95" "149.95" (European decimal comma to dot in numbers)
Embedded Line Break "Building A\nSuite 4" "Building A|Suite 4" (Dangerous newline replaced with pipe)
Date Standardization "24/09/2026" (DD/MM/YYYY) "2026-09-24" (Standardized to ISO-8601 via >12 rule)
⌨️
Validation Shortcuts: Press Ctrl+Shift+H to validate headers, Ctrl+Shift+P to validate data, or click Validate Date Format in the toolbar.
🛡️
Date Ambiguity Safeguard: When date parts cannot be determined with certainty (e.g. "03/05/2026" where both numbers are ≤ 12), NanoCell refuses to guess and warns you instead of risking data corruption.
⚙️
In Settings (Ctrl+G): You can customize validation rules to fit your database requirements:
  • Turn European decimal commas into dots for numeric values
  • Replace commas in text fields with dashes
  • Convert double quotes into single quotes
  • Replace line breaks inside cells with pipes (|)
  • Force all text to lowercase
🔒 100% Offline Privacy

100% Private: Your Data Never Leaves Your Computer

✨ "Oh, my sensitive customer records, payroll files, and company numbers never get uploaded to any external server?!"
The Pain Point

Uploading sensitive company records, financial numbers, or customer lists to online spreadsheet websites puts your private data at risk.

Most online tools send your files to their servers, where your data can be stored, analyzed, or accidentally exposed.

The NanoCell Solution

NanoCell runs 100% on your own computer inside your browser. No files are ever sent to an external server. Your data never leaves your machine.

Disconnect your Wi-Fi or turn on airplane mode—NanoCell runs completely offline. You can install it on your desktop without needing admin rights.

Security & Privacy Architecture 🛡️ Zero Cloud Roundtrips
Stage How It Works Privacy Guarantee
Opening Files Files are loaded directly from your hard drive into your browser No upload progress bar, no remote storage
Editing & Viewing All calculations and data changes happen in your computer's local memory Zero external web requests during use
Saving Files Writes the modified CSV directly back to your local folder Byte-exact file saved locally with zero cloud copies
💻
Desktop Installation: You can install NanoCell directly from your browser's address bar (click the install icon). It launches in its own dedicated window without browser toolbars, starts instantly, and works completely offline without needing admin privileges.
⌨️ The Essentials & Shortcuts

The Standard Essentials & Power Tools

✨ "Oh, I can transpose rows to columns, shift reorder data, and round numbers without touching the mouse?!"
Matrix Transposition

Pivots selected rows into columns and columns into rows. Ideal for re-orienting horizontal metric summaries for SQL ingestion.

Shortcut: Ctrl+Shift+T
Dynamic Row & Col Shift

Reorder columns or move rows up and down dynamically. No cutting, copying, or shifting adjacent cells around.

Shortcut: Alt + ↑ ↓ ← →
Series Auto-Expansion

Select your starting seed row and highlight rows below. Press Ctrl+E to project arithmetic progressions across the range.

Shortcut: Ctrl+E
In-Place Number Rounding

Round noisy floating-point calculations directly in the cells. No formula column writing or copy-paste-values needed.

Shortcuts: Ctrl+I (Int) • Ctrl+$ (2 Dec)
Freeze Header Row

Lock your column titles at the top with a single keystroke. Scroll through thousands of records without losing column context.

Shortcut: Ctrl+B
Fuzzy Command Palette

Developer-style quick launcher. Access every setting, formatter, and transformation via instant keyboard fuzzy search.

Shortcuts: Ctrl+K or Ctrl+P
Instant Date Stamp

Stamps today's date formatted as universal ISO YYYY-MM-DD directly into the active cell or selection.

Shortcut: Ctrl+;
Multi-Step Undo & Redo

Full transaction history with single-step rollback for batch validations and matrix transformations.

Shortcuts: Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y
Find & Occurrence Jump

Quick search across all cells; press Ctrl+D to jump straight to the next matching cell.

Shortcuts: Ctrl+F / Ctrl+D
Fast Column Sorting

Sort rows ascending or descending based on the active column while safely preserving the pinned header row.

Shortcuts: Ctrl+L / Ctrl+Shift+L
6 Built-In Themes

Light, Dark, Solarized, Night, Nord, and Dracula available in the settings dialog.

Shortcut: Settings (Ctrl+G)
Empty Row & Col Trimmer

Clean up bloated export files by automatically removing all trailing blank rows and columns.

Shortcut: Ctrl+Shift+T
⚙️
In Settings (Ctrl+G): You can customize your screen grid size (how many rows and columns fit on screen), highlight cells with hidden quotes or line returns in warning colors, and choose whether sorting keeps headers locked or sorts numbers before text.
